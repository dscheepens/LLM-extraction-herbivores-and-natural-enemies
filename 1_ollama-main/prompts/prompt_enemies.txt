### TL;DR (the “cheat‑sheet”)

1. **Identify the noun the control‑related clause attaches to.**  
2. **If the noun is “key”, “review”, “assemblage”, or a plural group → the statement applies to *all* taxa listed.**  
3. **If the noun is a single species name → the statement applies only to that species.**  
4. **Potential → Important = True, Biological = False.**  
5. **Proven control → Biological = True (and Important = True).**  
6. **No control mention → both flags = False.**
